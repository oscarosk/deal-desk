# Lumen Analytics contract playbook

> Fictional rules for a fictional company, written for the Flip the Switch demo.
> Not legal advice. `@legal` uses this playbook to flag clauses for a person to
> decide. It never approves a contract.

For each clause the customer asks for, `@legal` reports one of three outcomes:

- **Standard:** our own wording, so no approval is needed.
- **Fallback:** we can accept it, but only the finance lead can approve.
- **Never:** we can't accept it. `@legal` suggests the fallback instead.

## 1. Limitation of liability
- **Standard:** each side's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.
- **Fallback:** cap of 2× the fees paid in the previous 12 months.
- **Never:** unlimited or uncapped liability, or any cap above 2× annual fees.
- **Always excluded from the cap (standard):** confidentiality breaches and each side's indemnity obligations.

## 2. Indemnification
- **Standard:** we defend the customer against claims that our product infringes someone's intellectual property.
- **Fallback:** we also cover claims arising from our breach of the data protection terms, within the liability cap.
- **Never:** indemnity for the customer's own data, content, or misuse of the product.

## 3. Payment terms
- **Standard:** net 30, invoiced annually in advance.
- **Fallback:** net 45 or net 60, or quarterly invoicing.
- **Never:** net 90 or longer, or payment on acceptance.

## 4. Termination
- **Standard:** either side may terminate for an uncured material breach after 30 days' notice.
- **Fallback:** the customer may terminate for convenience at a renewal date, with 60 days' notice.
- **Never:** termination for convenience mid-term with a refund of prepaid fees.

## 5. Renewal and price increases
- **Standard:** renews automatically for the same term, with 60 days' notice to cancel, and price increases of up to 7% at renewal.
- **Fallback:** price increase capped at 5%.
- **Never:** a price freeze lasting more than one renewal.

## 6. Service levels
- **Standard:** 99.5% monthly uptime, with service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fee.
- **Fallback:** 99.9% uptime, with service credits of up to 15%.
- **Never:** credits above 25%, or a right to terminate for a single missed month.

## How `@legal` reports
For each requested change, it gives the clause number, the customer's wording in quotes, the outcome, and a suggested reply. It ends with the role that must approve, which is either "none" or "finance lead".
